## Reuse terms

CC BY requires attribution. CC BY-SA additionally requires that derivative works
of the image be shared under the same licence; displaying an unmodified photo
next to its credit, as this site does, does not make the site a derivative work.
Full terms are at the licence links above.

Replacing these with photographs of a real property means dropping the files
into `frontend/public/images`, keeping the same names, and updating this file.
